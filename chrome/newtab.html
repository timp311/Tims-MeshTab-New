Chrome only exposes New Tab ownership through the extension manifest. MeshTab can switch between MeshTab and Chrome's built-in New Tab screen, but Chrome does not expose the exact previous third-party New Tab extension for automatic restoration.

Turning a feature off only hides its MeshTab controls and panels. Your saved Notes, Tasks, Reminders, and Overlay definitions are kept. Quick Links (General Notes, Tasks, Reminders, Allocations, Overlays, Archive) always show while you're already on one of those screens; turning this on also shows them while you're on a regular Tab.
